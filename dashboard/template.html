Alberta grid analytics: forecasts, renewable value and congestion

Probabilistic forecasts of Alberta's load and pool price, tested on years the models never saw. Then the value of wind and solar as their share of the grid grows, and a screen of where transmission limits already hold generation back, ahead of the move to locational pricing. Built on 15 years of hourly public data from the Alberta Electric System Operator.
